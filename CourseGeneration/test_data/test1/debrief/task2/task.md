Once you've implemented rate limiting in your API, it's essential to handle responses correctly when limits are exceeded. In this task, you'll learn how to communicate with users when their requests are being throttled and the best practices to follow for crafting clear and informative error messages.

**Important Considerations:**
- HTTP status codes for rate limits (e.g., 429).
- Crafting response messages that inform users about when they can resume making requests.
- How to log and analyze rate limit hits for further optimization.
